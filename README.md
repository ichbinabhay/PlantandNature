# 🌿 Plants and Nature: The Living Foundation of Our World
### Academic Presentation & Interactive Website
**Course:** 1st Semester Professional Communication (PC)  
**Institution:** B.V. Bhoomaraddi College of Engineering & Technology (BVBCET)

---

## 📖 Overview
This interactive website transforms the speech and project **"Plants and Nature: The Living Foundation of Our World"** into a modern, engaging digital experience and classroom presentation hub. It explores the foundational role of plants in human survival, physical and mental wellbeing, ecological crises, and practical conservation stewardship.

---

## ✨ Features & Interactive Modules

1. **🌍 "A World Without Plants" Split Comparison Slider**
   - Interactive draggable slider comparing a lush, balanced biosphere against a barren, dystopian world without vegetation.
   - Dynamic real-time statistics (Atmospheric O₂, Surface Temperature, and Biodiversity).

2. **🌾 Everyday Essentials & Student Day Timeline**
   - Deep-dive cards into Food & Nutrition, Daily Materials (cotton, wood, paper), Medicine & Healing, and Shelter.
   - An interactive "Day in Your Life" timeline tracing plant interaction from morning coffee to nighttime rest.

3. **🔬 Interactive Photosynthesis Laboratory**
   - Real-time chemical synthesis simulator (`6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Sunlight ➔ C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂`).
   - Toggle Sun, CO₂, and Water inputs to observe biochemical reactions and chloroplast activity.

4. **🐝 Interconnected Ecological Web Simulator**
   - Test ecosystem fragility by simulating the loss of flowering plants and observing the cascading impact on pollinators, food crops, and humanity.

5. **🏙️ Urban Heat Island & Microclimate Simulator**
   - Slide tree canopy coverage from 0% to 100% to see surface street temperatures drop by up to 12°C.

6. **🎧 Procedural Nature Soundscape (Web Audio API)**
   - 100% offline procedural synthesizer generating gentle forest breeze and melodic songbird chirps.

7. **📜 Interactive Stewardship Pledge & Certificate**
   - Students check off green commitments, enter their name, and generate an official printable Certificate of Plant Stewardship.

8. **🎯 Classroom Knowledge Check (Interactive Quiz)**
   - 5-question interactive quiz testing core concepts from the speech with instant feedback and scoring.

9. **🖥️ Fullscreen Classroom Presentation Mode**
   - Dedicated projector/slide deck view for presenting in front of the class. Supports keyboard arrows (`←` / `→` / `Spacebar`) and `ESC` to exit.

10. **🗣️ Teleprompter & Speech Narration (Text-to-Speech)**
    - Verbatim speech transcript with automated browser speech synthesis, adjustable playback speed, and active paragraph highlighting.

11. **🍃 Ambient Falling Botanical Leaves**
    - High-performance HTML5 canvas particle system (toggleable with one click).

12. **🌙 Light & Dark Theme Support**
    - High-contrast, accessibility-friendly theme toggle with preference saved in `localStorage`.

## 🚀 How to Open and Run

No installations, build steps, or servers are required!

1. Open File Explorer to:
   ```
   D:\BVBCET 1st semester\PC\plants
   ```
2. Double-click **`index.html`** to launch it in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari).
3. To present in class, click the **"🖥️ Present"** button in the top navigation bar or press `ESC` at any time to exit slide mode.
4. To print or save a clean evaluation PDF, click **"🖨️ Print / Save PDF"** in the Speech section or press `Ctrl + P`.
